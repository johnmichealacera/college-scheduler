# DSPC Event Scheduling Guide

**ClassSync — Division of Siargao**  
Division Schools Press Conference (DSPC)

This guide is for coordinators and staff who will encode the DSPC contest timetable. Follow the steps in order. School class schedules and attendance are **not** affected.

---

## What this is for

Use **DSPC Event** to assign each contest to:

- a **facilitator**, or **TBA** if that person is still being arranged
- a **venue** (room)
- an **exact date** (example: September 9, 2026)
- a **time** (example: 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM)
- a **language** (English or Filipino)
- a **level** (Elementary or Secondary)

You can schedule the same contest more than once (for example, News Writing — English — Elementary at 1:00–3:00 PM, and News Writing — Filipino — Secondary later the same day).

---

## Before you start

1. Open ClassSync in your browser.
2. On the left menu, confirm you can see **Teachers**, **Rooms**, and **DSPC Event**.
3. If **DSPC Event** shows a yellow message about tables not being set up, stop and ask the system administrator to run the DSPC database setup. Do not encode slots until that message is gone.

**Important:** In this system, **Teachers** are also used as DSPC **facilitators**. You do not create a separate facilitator list.

---

## Step 1 — Add facilitators (when known)

Facilitators come from **Teachers**. You can encode contest slots first and choose **TBA (still to be arranged)** until names are confirmed.

When you know who will handle a contest:

1. Open **Teachers** in the left menu.
2. Click **Add Teacher**.
3. Type the facilitator’s full name (example: *Maria Santos*).
4. Click **Add Teacher**.

Repeat for every person who will handle a contest.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Edit a name | Click the pencil icon |
| Remove a facilitator | Click the trash icon only if that person is not needed anywhere. Removing them sets their DSPC slots to **TBA**. It also removes any regular class assignments they have. |

School teachers already in the list can be used as DSPC facilitators. You do not need to add them twice.

---

## Step 2 — Add venues

Venues are the rooms or halls where contests will be held.

1. Open **Rooms** in the left menu.
2. Click **Add Room**.
3. Type a clear venue name (examples: *Audio-Visual Room*, *SHS 2*, *Library*, *Covered Court*).
4. Click **Add Room**.

Repeat for every venue you will use on DSPC day(s).

Rooms used for regular classes can also be used as DSPC venues. DSPC bookings do **not** change class attendance. Do **not** delete a room that is still used for regular classes — that also removes its DSPC slots and class schedule entries.

---

## Step 3 — Open the DSPC timetable

1. Click **DSPC Event** in the left menu (under **Event**).
2. You should see the title **DSPC Schedule** and *Division of Siargao — Division Schools Press Conference*.

You can also open **Schedule**, then use the dropdown at the top right to switch from **School Classes** to **DSPC Event**.

The DSPC grid shows **calendar dates as columns** (Sep 9, Sep 10, …), not Monday–Friday. That is intentional.

---

## Step 4 — Add a contest slot

1. Click **Add Contest Slot**.
2. Fill in the fields below.
3. Click **Add to DSPC Schedule**.

### Fields to fill

| Field | What to choose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Contest** | One event from the official DSPC list | News Writing |
| **Language** | English or Filipino | English |
| **Level** | Elementary or Secondary | Secondary |
| **Facilitator** | A teacher, or **TBA (still to be arranged)** if not yet assigned | TBA, or Maria Santos |
| **Venue** | Room or hall | Audio-Visual Room |
| **Date** | Exact calendar date | 2026-09-09 (September 9, 2026) |
| **Start / End time** | Philippine time, between 7:00 AM and 9:00 PM | 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM |

Times use **Philippine Standard Time (GMT+8)**.

The facilitator field defaults to **TBA**. Leave it as TBA when the person is still being arranged. Change it later by clicking the slot and picking a teacher.

### Official contest list

Use only these contests:

1. News Writing  
2. Editorial Writing  
3. Editorial Cartooning  
4. Feature Writing  
5. Sci and Tech Writing  
6. Sports Writing  
7. Copyreading and Headline Writing  
8. Photojournalism  
9. Radio Broadcasting  
10. CDP  
11. Online Desktop  
12. TV Broadcasting  
13. Column Writing  

### Same contest, different groups

Create **separate slots** when language or level is different.

Example for one afternoon:

| Contest | Language | Level | Date | Time | Venue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| News Writing | English | Elementary | Sep 9, 2026 | 1:00–3:00 PM | AVR |
| News Writing | Filipino | Secondary | Sep 9, 2026 | 1:00–3:00 PM | Library |

Those two can run at the same time because they use different venues (and different facilitators).

### Same time on more than one date

If the same contest, facilitator, and venue should run on several dates at the same hours:

1. Fill the form as usual.
2. Click **Add another date**.
3. Pick the extra date(s).
4. Click **Add to DSPC Schedule**.

The system creates one slot per date.

---

## Step 5 — Check the timetable

After saving, a colored block appears on that date and time.

On each block you will see:

- contest name
- time (when the block is tall enough)
- facilitator
- venue

Hover a block to see the full details, including the full date (example: *Wednesday, September 9, 2026*).

---

## Edit or remove a slot

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Click the block, change the fields, click **Save Changes**. |
| **Delete** | Hover the block and click **×**, then confirm. |

Deleting a DSPC slot does **not** change school classes or attendance.

---

## Conflicts (double-booking)

You cannot save if:

- an **assigned facilitator** (not TBA) is already booked at that date and overlapping time, or
- the **same venue** is already booked at that date and overlapping time

Slots marked **TBA** do not conflict with each other on facilitator. They can still conflict if they share a venue.

A red warning appears before you save. Suggested free times are shown as buttons — click one to fill the time automatically.

On the timetable, conflicting blocks are highlighted in red.

DSPC does **not** block a room just because a regular class uses it on Mondays. DSPC and school schedules are separate. If a venue is needed for both, coordinate with the class scheduler so the room is actually free that calendar day.

---

## Filters (optional)

Above the grid you can narrow the view:

- **From / To** — show only slots in a date range  
- **Language** — English or Filipino  
- **Level** — Elementary or Secondary  
- **Contest / Facilitator / Venue** — pick one or more. Choose **TBA** in Facilitator to see slots still waiting for a name.  

Click **Clear filters** to see everything again.

Filters also apply when you export a PDF.

---

## Export the timetable (PDF)

1. Stay on **DSPC Event**.
2. Apply filters if you only want certain dates, levels, or venues.
3. Optional: check **Show vacant** to include unused venue times in the report.
4. Click **Export PDF**.

The file is a contest schedule for **Division of Siargao**, grouped by calendar date, with columns for Contest, Facilitator, and Venue.

## Worked example

**Goal:** Schedule News Writing (English, Secondary) on September 9, 2026, 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM, AVR, facilitator Maria Santos.

1. **Teachers** → add *Maria Santos* if she is not already listed.  
2. **Rooms** → add *AVR* if it is not already listed.  
3. **DSPC Event** → **Add Contest Slot**.  
4. Contest: **News Writing**.  
5. Language: **English**.  
6. Level: **Secondary**.  
7. Facilitator: **TBA (still to be arranged)** if the name is not yet known, or **Maria Santos** if it is.  
8. Venue: **AVR**.  
9. Date: **September 9, 2026**.  
10. Start **13:00**, End **15:00**.  
11. Click **Add to DSPC Schedule**.

The block should appear on the **Sep 9** column from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM.

---

## What DSPC scheduling does *not* do

- It does **not** mark students present or absent.  
- It does **not** change the regular **Schedule** (Monday–Sunday class timetable).  
- It does **not** add or rename school subjects.  
- It only stores DSPC contest slots.

---

## Quick checklist for management

- [ ] Facilitators who are known are encoded under **Teachers**; others are set to **TBA**  
- [ ] All venues are encoded under **Rooms**  
- [ ] Every contest needed that day has a slot (contest + language + level)  
- [ ] Dates are the actual event dates, not weekdays  
- [ ] No red conflict highlights remain  
- [ ] PDF exported and reviewed before printing or posting  

---

## If something looks wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No **DSPC Event** in the menu | Refresh the page. If it still missing, contact IT. |
| Yellow banner about SQL / tables | Contact the system administrator. Encoding will not save until setup is complete. |
| Saving with **TBA** fails | Ask the administrator to run `supabase/dspc_facilitator_optional.sql` in the Supabase SQL Editor, then refresh. |
| Facilitator does not appear in the dropdown | Add them first under **Teachers**, then return to DSPC Event. Until then, choose **TBA**. |
| Venue does not appear | Add it first under **Rooms**. |
| Cannot save | Read the red conflict message. Change time, venue, or facilitator, or click a suggested slot. |
| Time rejected | Use 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM only. End time must be after start time. |
| Wrong date on the grid | Click the block and change **Date**, then save. |

For regular class scheduling (subjects, Monday–Friday, attendance), use **Schedule** instead of **DSPC Event**. See `GUIDE.md` for that workflow.
